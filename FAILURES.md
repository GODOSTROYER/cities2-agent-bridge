## Open issues

- Large-building placement (Landfill01): needs occupancy-based site search for large footprints.
- Land exhaustion: needs a tile purchase strategy.
- Medium-density rezoning strategy is not yet designed.
- Editor tutorials are out of scope: the bridge requires a loaded city.
- Uncommitted working-tree changes (grid.ps1, play.ps1, lib/BridgeClient.psm1) hold the occupancy and demolition work for entry 14. Commit or review before relying on it.
- `get_services` `buildings` lists the service's prefab types, not placed buildings; do not use it as a building count. The `services` MCP tool reports that array's length as `buildings`.
- Service upkeep total (~265k) exceeds the sum of placed service-building prefab upkeep (~150k at 100%). Source not yet attributed (roads or parks?).
- find_free_lots takes only the 20 closest-link finalists before terrain checks (17 of 20 were wet in one query); widen when it returns none.
- upkeep attribution: ServiceUpkeep total still exceeds placed service-building upkeep.
